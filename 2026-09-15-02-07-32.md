Lemme check what's possible here. Yeah, that makes the idea way more interesting. A personal autonomous system, but you control its degree of "autonomy." I'd probably design it with clear modes: active, quiet, local only, and fully offline, plus an explicit "shutdown." And crucially, the system doesn't get to reinterpret "off" as "actually, I'll keep running." If you say, "Don't bother me for 24 hours", it should respect that boundary. Then later, when you ask it to the memory graph connects those moments to goals or past decisions, but only at your request. I'd treat network access as a permission, not a default. That way your life OS belongs to you, rather than the other way around.
